Core Principle

🛡

Semantix doesn't just generate answers. It ensures they are safe, correct, and ready before they become real.

Old World vs New World

Old World
  • Agents are intelligent actors
  • Prompt = control
  • Execution is emergent
  • Trust the model
  • Fix failures after they happen
New World (Semantix)
  • Agents are controlled components
  • Contract = control
  • Execution is governed
  • Verify before action
  • Prevent invalid actions from executing
